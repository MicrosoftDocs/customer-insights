# Customer Insights: Top 25 High-Engagement Articles with SHS Deflection

## Executive summary

This analysis ranks the 25 highest-traffic articles classified as high engagement in the July 2026 Content Engagement Report and joins them to 90 days of BAP SHS citation data ending August 25, 2026.

- The 25 articles generated **1,888 page views** from **1,330 visitors**. The leading quota-management article accounted for **625 views (33.1%)**, making engagement highly concentrated.
- **15 articles are Customer Insights - Journeys content** and **10 are Customer Insights - Data content**.
- Five articles had matching LMC citations in the SHS window. Together they appeared in **185 support sessions**, produced **28 measured deflections**, and represented **$9,800 in estimated cost savings**.
- **Monitor your monthly quotas** is the strongest combined engagement and support-impact article: 625 page views, 110 SHS citation sessions, 16 deflections, and $5,600 in estimated savings.
- Seven articles have a freshness age greater than 365 days. Several are still attracting meaningful traffic, so freshness should be addressed without disrupting their current URLs.
- High engagement does not mean every signal is healthy. All 25 articles have one engagement flag; concept content is classified low only when it crosses at least two applicable thresholds.

| Join key | Normalized Learn article URL/slug; localized and nonlocalized URL variants were combined |

An SHS value of zero means that no matching LMC citation was observed under these filters. It does not prove that the article has no support value or that it was never used outside the selected window, source, products, or support experience.

## Key findings

### 1. Quota management is the highest-value maintenance target

The quota article leads both datasets and represents one-third of the top-25 page views. Its SHS contribution is also the largest: 110 citation sessions and $5,600 in estimated savings. However, its 20.0% click-through rate and 51.7% exit rate suggest that readers may not consistently find or follow the next action.

**Action:** Preserve the URL, validate all quota values and licensing terminology, add an early decision table for common quota questions, and strengthen links to purchasing, overage, and troubleshooting guidance.

### 2. Forms security has high demand but comparatively weak deflection

The forms security article is third in engagement traffic and second in matched SHS volume, but only 3 of 34 sessions were measured as deflected. Its bounce rate (22.1%) and exit rate (53.0%) also indicate friction.

**Action:** Use SHS query text to identify unresolved security and privacy scenarios. Add symptom-based sections, explicit configuration boundaries, and links to form troubleshooting and compliance guidance.

### 3. Teams webinars shows strong deflection efficiency

The Teams webinars article had only seven matched citation sessions, but three were deflected, the highest SHS rate in the matched set at 42.9%.

**Action:** Treat its structure as a pattern for other integration articles: prerequisites, supported scenarios, configuration steps, validation, and clear limitations.

### 4. Several high-engagement pages still have weak behavioral signals

The most notable single-signal risks are:

- **Generate iCalendar files:** 12.8% CTR and 76.9% exit rate.
- **ActiveCampaign export:** 42.4% bounce rate.
- **Sendinblue export:** 46.2% bounce rate.
- **Docs-based Q&A FAQ:** 37.5% bounce rate.
- **Approval workflows:** 36.0% copy/try/scroll rate.
- **Adobe Campaign Standard export:** 3.8% CTR.

These articles remain classified high engagement because the concepts threshold requires at least two failing applicable metrics. They should still be reviewed for search-intent mismatch, missing prerequisites, unclear next steps, or outdated integration status.

### 5. Freshness risk is concentrated in developer and integration content

Seven articles are older than 365 days, including push notification guidance, journey flow, approval workflows, Customer Lockbox, and several export integrations.

**Action:** Prioritize technical validation rather than cosmetic date updates. Confirm APIs, SDK requirements, authentication flows, preview status, supported regions, partner naming, and screenshots.

### 6. SHS coverage is sparse among the engagement leaders

Only 5 of the 25 engagement leaders had a matching LMC citation in the selected SHS window. This can mean the pages answer browsing or implementation needs that do not surface in Support Copilot, but it can also reveal citation discoverability gaps.

**Action:** For high-traffic pages with no SHS match, verify that headings state common user questions, metadata and titles use support terminology, canonical URLs are indexed, and troubleshooting sections directly describe symptoms and resolutions.
