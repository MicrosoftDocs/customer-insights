# Monitor your quota usage

See how many unified profiles your organization can use for Customer Insights - Data, and how many are used across all the Customer Insights - Data instances in the tenant. The quota page is only visible to users who have access to Customer Insights - Data. For general information on licensing, see [Customer Insights license guidance](../journeys/license-setup.md).

To check your current quota usage and limits, go to **Settings** > **Quota usage**.

:::image type="content" source="media/quota-limits.png" alt-text="Screenshot of the Quota usage page for monitoring current usage and limits.":::

## Frequently asked questions

### What happens if you are over your quota?

Small and brief overages are generally ignored, but organizations that are consistently and significantly processing more data than they licensed might see suspensions to data processing.

### What should you do when you are over your quota?

If you are at or above your licensed quota, choose one of the following options:

- Reduce your usage to fall within your licensed quota for the next month.
- Ask the person in your organization that handles licenses to increase your licensed quota.

### I don't recognize an instance shown

Switch to a different Customer Insights - Data environments in your tenant. Select the **Environment picker** in the header of the app to switch to another environment.

### I don't have permission to access an environment

Ask someone in your organization with a Power Platform System Administrator Role to sign in to the instance.

### I want to delete an environment that's no longer needed

In the environment switcher, select the vertical three dots next to the environment name, and then select **Delete**.
