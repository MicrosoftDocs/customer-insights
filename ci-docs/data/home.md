# Explore the user interface

Access [Dynamics 365 Customer Insights - Data](https://home.ci.ai.dynamics.com/) at [https://home.ci.ai.dynamics.com/](https://home.ci.ai.dynamics.com/).

The **Home** page guides you through the configuration process for key features and provides an overview of segments, measures, and enrichment data.

:::image type="content" source="media/home-page.png" alt-text="Screenshot of the Home screen.":::

## Left side pane

Use the left pane to navigate between different areas of Customer Insights - Data.

## Application header

The application header provides the following controls:

- **Environment** picker: Shows the environment you work in and lets you create or manage environments as an administrator.

- **Feedback** control: Select the smiley face icon to provide feedback about your experience. The product team actively listens to your feedback and appreciates it when you share what you like and how they can improve.

- **Settings** control: Select the gear icon to gather session details and configure global settings for your Microsoft 365 profile.

- **Notifications** options: Select the bell icon to see the latest updates and announcements.

- **Help** options: Select the question mark icon to get contextual help links and other helpful resources.

- **Your profile picture**: Opens the **Account manager** for your Microsoft 365 profile. Select **My account** to manage your personal settings.

## Getting started with Customer Insights - Data

This section contains cards that help you walk through the process of setting up your environment.

1. The **Add data** card assists you with your data import. Customer Insights - Data supports [several options to bring in data about your customers](data-sources.md). Select **Add data sources** to get started.
1. After the initial data import successfully completes, use the **Unify data** card to harmonize the data and [create unified customer profiles](data-unification.md) from disparate sources.
1. With unified customer profiles in place, review the **Analyze data** options to get more insights. Try creating [business measures](measures.md) to track KPIs, [define segments](segments.md) to reach specific audiences, or [configure predictions](predictions.md) with the help of AI.
1. Now that you imported, unified, and neatly structured your customer data, use the [various export destinations](export-destinations.md) to take action on the data. Select **Add connections** to connect your favorite services. For example, export the data to Dynamics 365 Customer Insights - Journeys to create marketing campaigns and build customer journeys.

## Your customer insights section

- **Segments** shows groups of customers based on demographic, behavioral, or transactional attributes that you defined. [Create segments](segments.md) to group your customer base and better target your business activities.

- **Business measures** shows tiles with [key performance indicators (KPIs)](measures.md) that you defined. For example, average likelihood of a customer to churn or the average online spend per customer.

- **Enrichments** lists results of the enrichment runs that completed recently. [Enrichments](enrichment-manage.md) add information about your customer base. For example, by understanding the interests and brands that they have affinity for.
