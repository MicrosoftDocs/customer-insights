The website tracking script uses the following visitor cookie. This table doesn't provide a complete inventory of cookies from other features or scripts on your website.

| Cookie name | Is essential | Purpose | First or third party | Properties | When set |
|-------------|--------------|---------|----------------------|------------|----------|
| `_msci` | No | Stores a visitor identifier used by the website tracking script. | First party | Persistent, client-side; configured expiry of 365 days when written. | Initially created when the website tracking script initializes, no existing visitor cookie is available, and browser storage and cookie usage are permitted. |

The configured expiry starts when a cookie is written. Browser policies, cookie deletion, and your website's consent controls can shorten a cookie's availability.
